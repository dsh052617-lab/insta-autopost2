Key takeaways
- Decart’s technology will help Amazon further optimize both its inference and custom silicon performance so more customers can train and run AI models faster and at lower cost.
- Since 2025, Decart's team has worked closely with Amazon to deliver strong performance results on Trainium, using a full-stack approach that spans software and hardware.
Today, Amazon announced it has signed an agreement to purchase the proprietary technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack (DOS), and bring the Decart AI co-founders—Dean Leitersdorf, Moshe Shalev, and Orian Leitersdorf—along with their team of AI researchers and engineers, to Amazon. With their deep expertise in full-stack performance optimization across hardware and software, Decart brings a strong connection between technology and talent that will help further Amazon’s investment in custom silicon and optimize AI inference for its customers.
Amazon has long believed that inference speed and compute efficiency are central to the future of AI, and has advanced that vision through innovation across its custom silicon portfolio, from Graviton to Trainium. That work spans large language models, multimodal and agentic systems, and world models, alike. Amazon began working with Decart in 2025, and the collaboration quickly showed how much the two teams shared, from a full-stack view of performance to an appetite for solving some of the world’s most challenging science and engineering problems.
“Every leap forward in AI starts with getting more out of every chip,” said Peter DeSantis, SVP of foundational AI models, custom silicon, quantum computing, Amazon. “More efficient compute means better training, better reinforcement learning, faster inference, and better models for customers at a lower cost. That's the pillar Trainium and our AI efforts are built on, and from our earliest work with Decart, we knew they were the kind of technologically fearless team who would help us push it even further.”
“From day one, our mission has been to redefine the boundaries of what AI can do and to make it dramatically more efficient along the way,” said Dean Leitersdorf, co-founder and CEO of Decart. “Joining Amazon gives our team the scale, the customers, and the infrastructure to pursue that mission faster than we could on our own. We’re looking forward to exploring what we can build together.”
Completion of the transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory clearance.
