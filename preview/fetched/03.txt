Gemini 4 Argon: Intelligence, Performance & Price Analysis
Model summary
Gemini 4 Argon (High) is above average in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.
Gemini 4 Argon (High) scores 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 41). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 113M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 77.3M.
Pricing for Gemini 4 Argon (High) is $2.00 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.00) and $10.00 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $4.97 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Argon (High) on the Intelligence Index.
At 30.9 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Gemini 4 Argon (High) is notably slow compared with the median of 13.3 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).
